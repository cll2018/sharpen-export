<p>*Release date: 2024-08-16* Recently, the Science and Technology Department of Hunan Province announced the list of technology-based SMEs planned for inclusion in the first batch for 2024; our company was again approved for inclusion. As the core strength driving innovation and technological development, technology-based SMEs specifically refer to SMEs that rely on a certain number of scientific and technological personnel to carry out scientific and technological research activities, successfully obtain independent intellectual property, and successfully convert them into high-tech products or services to achieve continuous and steady growth. Technology-based SMEs play an important role in promoting economic development, promoting employment, and accelerating scientific and technological progress, and can enjoy support policies provided by the national and local governments. Technology-based SMEs have high technical content and strong innovation capabilities, and use scientific and technological innovation to lead the development of new quality productive forces, achieving a high level of independent and strong scientific and technological capabilities; they are an important force in cultivating and developing new growth drivers and promoting high-quality development of the economy and society. Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd., established in 2013, has been deeply engaged in the field of new powder metallurgy materials, and is a national high-tech enterprise integrating R&D, production, sales, and technical services, a Hunan Province 'Little Giant' enterprise for specialization, refinement, difference, and innovation, a Changsha City smart manufacturing pilot enterprise, a Hunan Province new materials enterprise, and a first-batch innovative SME in Hunan Province. It has always adhered to the concept of 'scientific and technological innovation driving high-quality economic development', relying on a top-tier PhD R&D team led by Professor He Yuehui of the Powder Metallurgy Research Institute of Central South University, who is a specially appointed professor of the Yangtze River Scholars program, a recipient of the State Council's special government allowance, a recipient of the first prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, and a recipient of the National Outstanding Youth Fund, to focus on solving 'bottleneck' technical problems in new materials, realizing the domestic substitution of imported products. It has established deep technical cooperation and exchange with Central South University, Xiangtan University, City University of Hong Kong, and other universities, possessing strong scientific and technological innovation and achievement transformation capabilities.</p>
