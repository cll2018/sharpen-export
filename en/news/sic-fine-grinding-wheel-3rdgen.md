<p>*Publish date: 2026-01-01* In the wave of the semiconductor industry upgrading to third-generation semiconductor materials, silicon carbide wafers, with their excellent properties such as high thermal conductivity and high breakdown voltage, exhibit significant advantages under extreme operating conditions such as high temperature, high voltage, and high frequency, and are expected to reshape trillion-level markets in new energy vehicles, photovoltaics, energy storage, etc. With the rise of third-generation semiconductor materials represented by silicon carbide, the processing technology of silicon carbide and other high-hardness, high-brittleness wafers has always been one of the "chokehold" problems in the domestic semiconductor industry. Relying on technical breakthroughs and market validation after more than ten years of deep cultivation in the new materials field, the R&D team of Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd. has achieved a major technical breakthrough in the field of silicon carbide wafer grinding. The core new product independently developed—precision grinding wheel for thinning silicon carbide wafers—meets industry needs in core indicators. The company's R&D team uses highly brittle light metal oxide composites as the bond for diamond grinding wheels, ensuring the wheel has high sharpness, achieving large feed and rapid grinding, while avoiding metal ion contamination; through ultra-high porosity self-generated pore technology, the wheel is endowed with high chip capacity and chip evacuation capability, avoiding wheel clogging and workpiece surface burn; 30000# ultra-fine diamond ensures excellent grinding quality, obtaining ultra-high gloss grinding surfaces, with silicon carbide surface roughness within 2 nm; the wheel has excellent performance and good stability, with no chipping or tooth breakage phenomena during long-term use. The product application field is mainly used for the thinning of SiC substrates, back thinning of SiC devices, discrete devices, integrated circuit substrate silicon wafers, and raw silicon wafers, etc. The series of Sharpen New Materials silicon carbide wafer thinning grinding wheels has served several domestic third-generation semiconductor silicon carbide substrate processing enterprises, and provides customized overall solutions. In the hard-fought battle of science, technology and innovation for grinding wheels used in semiconductor wafer thinning, Sharpen New Materials uses tenacious will and tireless innovative spirit as sharp blades, in the trumpet call of the times, strives to take the lead, overcoming a series of key technical difficulties, successfully realizing the scaled production of grinding wheels for semiconductor wafer thinning, providing strong and solid technical support for the development of China's semiconductor industry.</p>
