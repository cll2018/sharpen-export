<p>*Release date: 2016-11-07* Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd., as a supplier and service provider of technical solutions for high-speed high-quality grinding systems, owns complete independent intellectual property rights, and is a specialized high-technology enterprise providing overall technical solutions for grinding and polishing processing of products such as cemented carbide and high-speed steel rotors and cutting tools. The company has strong technical forces, strong product development capabilities, skilled technical processes, and high-level process products. Currently, a certain Shanghai enterprise uses the diamond grinding wheels produced by our company for mass production of three-blade tapered milling cutters with a diameter of 28mm and a cutting edge length of 50mm. The milling cutters produced achieve a maximum single-side cutting depth of 5.5mm, and the average feed speed of the grinding wheel increased by more than 30% year-on-year compared to a certain brand of grinding wheel in South Korea. Changsha Sharpen New Materials Company independently develops special adhesives for superhard material grinding wheels, combined with preparation technology, and the diamond grinding wheels produced have the following obvious advantages in structure and performance: 1. The working layer of the grinding wheel has both high chip holding and chip removal performance and high rotational strength; 2. The grinding wheel has both high sharpness and shape retention; 3. The grinding wheel substrate has the performance characteristics of light weight, high thermal conductivity, and environmental corrosion resistance; 4. The working layer and substrate of the grinding wheel achieve full metallurgical bonding, having very high interface stability; 5. The physical and mechanical properties of the entire grinding wheel are highly matched.</p>
