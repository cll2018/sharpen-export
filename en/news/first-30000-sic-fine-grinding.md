<p>*Release date: 2024-06-07* The core step in the silicon carbide production process is the processing of silicon carbide substrates, which is mainly divided into three processes: silicon carbide substrate cutting, thinning, and polishing. Thinning is mainly achieved through grinding and lapping; lapping is further divided into coarse grinding and fine grinding. The SiC wafer thinning wheel and grinding technology independently developed by Sharpen New Materials, from the wafer damage mechanism during the grinding process, to the coarse and fine grinding thinning of the wafer, uses a diamond grinding wheel to achieve wafer thinning processing with low damage and high cutting rate.
