<p>*Release date: 2016-11-07* Diamond products must not be rolled during storage to avoid cracks and surface damage; they must not be subjected to strong vibration and impact. Grooved wheels are manufactured with high precision; the electroplating process can stably produce high-precision, complex-surface grooved wheels, with a service life reaching 2 to 50,000 runs. The storage time for grinding wheels must not exceed the shelf life of the grinding wheel; grinding wheels with resin and rubber bond, if stored for more than one year from the date of leaving the factory, must pass a rotation test before use. Diamond tools for processing hard and brittle materials are mainly various diamond saws and diamond products; although the application scope and processing characteristics of various tools are different, their wear mechanisms are generally the same. The storage site for grinding wheels should remain dry, with a suitable temperature, avoiding being placed together with other chemicals, and preventing the grinding wheels from being moistened, exposed to low temperatures, overheated, or eroded by harmful chemicals, which would reduce strength.</p>
