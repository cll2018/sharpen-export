<p>*Release date: 2016-11-07* Looking at the development of the grinding field, future grinding processing will impose higher requirements on abrasives and grinding wheels. Judging from the current situation, superhard products precisely meet these new grinding needs. For example, CBN abrasive has characteristics such as good thermal stability, high hardness, and good wear resistance. Therefore, its grinding wheel processing has high linear speed, high grinding efficiency, and long grinding wheel life, making it particularly suitable for processing ferrous metal materials such as high-speed steel, bearing steel, stainless steel, and chilled cast iron. The application of superhard grinding wheels mainly refers to the application of grinding wheels made with resin, ceramic, or metal bond, using superhard materials such as metal powder, metal oxides, or CBN as fillers. Currently, the high precision and high efficiency grinding effects brought by superhard grinding wheels on grinding discs have been widely recognized. The emergence of new types of abrasives and grinding wheels, such as ceramic microcrystalline abrasives composed of micron-level polycrystalline structures, hollow shell abrasives containing fine diamond grit, and polyester film belts for ultra-fine polishing, etc. The characteristics of these new types of abrasives and grinding wheels make their grinding processing advantages fully displayed.</p>
