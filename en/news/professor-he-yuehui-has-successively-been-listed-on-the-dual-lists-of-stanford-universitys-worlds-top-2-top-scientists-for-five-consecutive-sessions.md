Global Top 2% Scientists Ranking Updated
Local time October 7, the team of Professor John P.A. Ioannidis from Stanford University, USA, jointly with Elsevier, released the 9th edition of the World's Top 2% Scientists Ranking (World's Top 2% Scientists 2026).

Congratulations to Professor Yuehui He, Chief Scientist at Sharpen New Materials, who has been selected again, making it the fifth consecutive time he has appeared on both the Career-long Impact and the Single-year Impact rankings. This highlights his sustained high-level academic influence in the fields of powder metallurgy and materials science. The ranking relies on the Scopus citation database, without any application or selection process, and is based on bibliometric indicators, screening scholars in the top 2% of various disciplines from among millions of researchers worldwide. It is divided into two main sections: the Career-long Impact and the Single-year Impact. Professor Yuehui He has long been deeply involved in the research of powder metallurgy, refractory metals, and new intermetallic compound materials, and multiple research achievements have achieved engineering applications. Being selected for both rankings for the fifth consecutive time is high recognition from the international academic community for his continuous output of high-quality research results. By being selected for this list, scientists receive global recognition for advancing knowledge and influencing their scientific communities. This is an important resource for recognizing outstanding achievements and tracking cutting-edge scientific contributions. Congratulations to all researchers who have been recognized for their outstanding contributions in 2026. Your dedication drives progress and innovation worldwide!
