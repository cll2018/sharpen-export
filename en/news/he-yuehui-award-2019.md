<p>*Release date: 2019-11-14* On the morning of November 14, 2019, the opening ceremony of the Second China New Materials Industry Development Conference was held at the Hunan International Convention and Exhibition Center (Mango Hall), and Professor He Yuehui received the "New Materials Transformation Award" from this conference and attended the award ceremony. Professor He Yuehui mainly researches the direction of "intermetallic compounds", applying new concept materials of intermetallic compounds to the upgrade of traditional materials, the expansion of concepts in traditional material fields, the proposal of new materials for new structures and the overtaking in the curve of new material performance. Based on research results such as preparing porous materials by biased diffusion pore formation based on the Kirkendall effect, achieving ferroalloys, high-titanium slag, Mn-Si alloy submerged arc furnaces, MoS roasting, and innovative new methods for dry process production of yellow phosphorus, he founded Chengdu E-Tai Technology Co., Ltd., directly creating economic benefits of several billion yuan. Professor He Yuehui took the lead in conducting research on preparation and application technology of cemented carbide in China, founded Changsha Mei She Rui New Materials Co., Ltd., specializing in the production of Ti(C,N)-based cermet materials and their products. The products were successfully applied to enterprises such as Chery Automobile, adding 1.2 billion yuan in economic benefits for them, and powerfully promoting the production technology transformation of China's mechanical manufacturing industry. Synchronously developing diamond wire with Nippon Kinka and forming industrialization, realizing clean, efficient, and low-cost processing of solar silicon wafers. In 2017, Changsha Dale New Materials Co., Ltd., founded by He Yuehui as an initiator, was listed on the ChiNext, directly creating output value of several billion yuan and indirect value of over 10 billion yuan. Relying on the technology applications developed by He Yuehui, two high-tech enterprises, Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd. and Hunan Province Kejia Materials Co., Ltd., and Chengdu Guoheng New Materials Co., Ltd. and Hunan Province Youliwei Technology Co., Ltd. were also founded, creating billions of yuan in value for society every year. The Second China New Materials Industry Development Conference was initiated and hosted by the China Materials Research Association, aiming to serve the national new materials development strategy, serve new materials specialty industries, serve new materials innovation and entrepreneurship, and help the healthy and rapid development of China's new materials industry. The "New Materials Transformation Award" was first established at this conference, awarded to experts and scholars who have made outstanding contributions in the innovation and industrialization process of new materials.</p>
