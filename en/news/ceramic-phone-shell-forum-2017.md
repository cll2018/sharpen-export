<p>*Release Date: 2017-03-17* On March 17, 2017, EBang Smart Manufacturing held in Shenzhen the 《Second Powder Metallurgy / Mobile Phone Ceramic Shell Technology and Application Forum and Exhibition》, company representatives were invited to attend the meeting. This seminar and exhibition mainly revolves around metal powder injection molding and its related technology applications, and the research themes of ceramic shell manufacturing technology and applications for smartphones. Company Chief Project Expert Professor He Yuehui presented on the theme of 《Superhard Material Grinding Wheels and Application Technology for High Efficiency and High Precision Grinding and Polishing of Zirconia Ceramic Pieces》. Based on the special properties and technical parameters of zirconia ceramic material, combined with the processing theory of intermetallic compound bond superhard material grinding wheels, after years of thematic research and development by the company and repeated practice of more than three thousand formulas, a high efficiency and high precision grinding and polishing solution for zirconia ceramics was obtained, and it has been successfully and maturely applied to the processing fields of zirconia ceramic smartphone back panels and fingerprint recognition pieces, greatly improving processing efficiency and reducing production costs for customers. The successful holding of this seminar will promote the development of the company in the field of zirconia ceramic smartphone grinding and polishing and its scaled application.</p>
