# About Sharpen New Materials

Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd. (长沙市萨普新材料有限公司) was founded in 2013 and is dedicated to the field of powder metallurgy new materials. We are an integrated national high-tech enterprise combining R&D, manufacturing, sales and technical service — a Hunan Province specialized & innovative "Little Giant" enterprise, a national technology-based and innovative enterprise, and a Changsha intelligent-manufacturing pilot enterprise.

## R&D Strength

Our team is built around the Changjiang Scholars innovation team led by Professor He Yuehui of Central South University, with 8 PhDs and Masters in materials science. The team has filed **37 patents** — 13 authorized invention patents, 4 utility-model patents, and 20 invention patents under review — and maintains deep technical cooperation with Central South University, Xiangtan University and City University of Hong Kong. With 30+ years of research in powder metallurgy and new materials, the team commands advanced PM tool-material preparation equipment and know-how.

## Core Technologies & Products

- **Diamond & CBN grinding wheels** for tool grinding (carbide, high-speed steel, cermet)
- **Diamond wheel discs** for thinning and polishing of hard-brittle materials (SiC, sapphire, mono-/poly-crystalline silicon, zirconia)
- **TiNiCo heat spreaders** for optical-glass precision molding
- **Powder metallurgy high-speed steel** for tools and dies

Our high-performance diamond and CBN wheels feature high sharpness, excellent shape retention and easy dressing / long life — ideal for large (Ø10 mm+) carbide and PM-HSS rotary tools, Ti(C,N) cermet tools, PCD and PCBN inserts.

## Applications & Customers

Products serve precision machinery, semiconductor, optics, solar PV, automotive and mold processing. We supply 200+ enterprises at home and abroad — including Tonly (东尼电子), Biel Crystal (伯恩光学) and Focus Lightings (聚灿光电) — and our products have entered the supply chains of Huawei, vivo and oppo.

## Vision

We are committed to solving "chokepoint" technical problems in new materials and realizing domestic substitution of imported products. By integrating materials-industry technology and supply-chain resources, we aim to build a globally leading materials-technology win-win platform.
