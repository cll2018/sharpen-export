    <h2>Silicon wafer thinning diamond grinding wheel</h2>
    <p>The silicon wafer thinning diamond grinding wheel is a core specialized consumable for the precision thinning process of the back side of semiconductor wafers, fully compliant with the GB/T 44687-2024 national industry standard for superhard abrasive products, specifically designed for high-precision thickness reduction of large-size silicon wafers of 12 inches and below.</p>
    <h3>Technical parameters</h3>
    <ul class="specs">
      <li><strong>Ultra-high processing precision</strong>: The total thickness variation (TTV) of 12-inch silicon wafer processing can be stably controlled within 1μm, meeting the flatness requirements of ultra-thin wafers for advanced packaging.</li>
      <li><strong>Extreme cost reduction and efficiency increase</strong>: Coarse grinding feed speed can reach 1.2μm/s, with grinding efficiency increased by 30%-50% compared to conventional products; long-life design significantly reduces wheel replacement frequency, substantially lowering comprehensive production line consumable costs.</li>
      <li><strong>Low load and low damage</strong>: Optimized diamond abrasive layout structure, lower grinding processing load, effectively reducing wafer edge chipping and sub-surface damage, lowering processing pressure for subsequent processes.</li>
      <li><strong>Full process adaptation</strong>: Workpiece surface roughness Ra after fine grinding can reach nanometer level, significantly reducing the processing margin for subsequent CMP polishing, shortening the processing cycle of the entire production line.</li>
      <li><strong>Flexible selection of multiple formulations</strong>: Provides metal, resin, and ceramic mainstream bond products, respectively adapted to the differentiated needs of different process stages such as coarse grinding, fine grinding, and ultra-fine grinding.</li>
      <li><strong>Stable mass production guarantee</strong>: Strict full-process quality control system ensures product batch consistency, significantly reducing debugging time after production line changeovers, directly improving the final yield of the entire production line.</li>
    </ul>
