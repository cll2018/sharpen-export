    <h2>Silicon Wafer Thinning Diamond Grinding Wheel</h2>
    <p>Silicon wafer thinning diamond grinding wheel is the core dedicated consumable for the precision backside thinning process of semiconductor wafers, fully compliant with the GB/T 44687-2024 national industry standard for superabrasive products, designed specifically for high-precision thickness reduction of 12-inch and below large-size silicon wafers.</p>
    <h3>Technical Parameters</h3>
    <ul class="specs">
      <li><strong>Ultra-high machining accuracy</strong>: The total thickness variation (TTV) for 12-inch silicon wafer machining can be stably controlled within 1μm, meeting the flatness requirements of ultra-thin wafers for advanced packaging.</li>
      <li><strong>Extreme cost reduction and efficiency improvement</strong>: The rough grinding feed rate can reach 1.2μm/s, with grinding efficiency improved by 30%-50% compared to regular products; the long-life design significantly reduces wheel replacement frequency, significantly reducing the comprehensive consumable cost of the production line.</li>
      <li><strong>Low load, low damage</strong>: Optimized diamond abrasive layout structure, lower grinding processing load, effectively reducing edge chipping and sub-surface damage at wafer edges, reducing the processing pressure of subsequent processes.</li>
      <li><strong>Full process adaptability</strong>: After fine grinding, the surface roughness Ra of the workpiece can reach the nanometer level, significantly reducing the machining allowance for subsequent CMP polishing, shortening the processing cycle of the entire production line.</li>
      <li><strong>Flexible selection of multiple formulations</strong>: Provides mainstream bond products in three categories: metal, resin, and ceramic, respectively adapted to the differentiated needs of different process stages such as rough grinding, fine grinding, and ultra-fine grinding.</li>
      <li><strong>Stable mass production guarantee</strong>: A strict full-process quality control system ensures batch consistency of the product, significantly reducing the debugging time after production line changeover, directly improving the final yield of the entire production line.</li>
    </ul>
