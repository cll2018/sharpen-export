<p>*Publication date: 2024-06-07* The core step in the silicon carbide production process is the processing of silicon carbide substrates, which is mainly divided into three processes: cutting, thinning, and polishing of silicon carbide substrates. Among them, thinning is mainly achieved through grinding and milling, and milling is divided into coarse grinding and fine grinding. The SiC wafer thinning wheel and grinding technology independently developed by Sharpen New Materials, from the wafer damage mechanism in the grinding process to the coarse and fine grinding thinning of wafers, uses diamond grinding wheels to achieve low-damage and high cutting rate wafer thinning processing. Changsha Sharpen New Materials Co., Ltd. was established in 2013 and is a national high-tech enterprise focused on the field of new materials in powder metallurgy, integrating R&D, production, sales, and technical services, a Hunan Province 'Little Giant' specialized and innovative enterprise, a Changsha smart manufacturing pilot enterprise, a Hunan Province new materials enterprise, and a first-batch innovative SME in Hunan Province. It always upholds the concept of 'scientific and technological innovation driving high-quality economic development', relying on a highly skilled doctoral R&D team led by Professor He Yuehui of the Powder Metallurgy Research Institute of Central South University, who is a specially appointed professor of the Changjiang Scholar, a recipient of the special government allowance of the State Council, a first-prize recipient of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, and a recipient of the National Outstanding Youth Fund, and is committed to solving the 'chokehold' technical problems of new materials and achieving the domestic replacement of imported products.</p>
